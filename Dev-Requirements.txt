Project Requirements: Travel Recommendation Web Application

1. CORE PAGES & NAVIGATION
- Home Page: Introduction to the platform and hero section.
- About Us Page: Company information and team membership guidance.
- Contact Us Page: Contact info and email inquiry form.
- Navigation Bar Elements:
  - Home Link
  - About Us Link
  - Contact Us Link
  - Search Input Box
  - Search Button
  - Clear Button

2. FUNCTIONALITY
- Search System: Implement keyword-based search for "beach", "temple", and "country".
- Data Source: Fetch travel data from travel_data.json.
- Dynamic Display: Show at least two recommendations with images for each category.
- State Management: Ability to clear results and reset the view.

3. DATASET REQUIREMENTS
- Beaches: Bora Bora, Copacabana, Clearwater Beach FL, Miami Beach FL, Lanikai Beach Hawaii.
- Temples: Taj Mahal, Angkor Wat.
- Countries: 
  - Australia (Sydney, Melbourne)
  - Japan (Tokyo, Kyoto)
  - Brazil (Sao Paulo, Rio de Janeiro)
